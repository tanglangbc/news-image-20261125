Entry for the make.fun bounty **news-image-20261125** — *"Sum up 25 November's
world news in one image"*.

One 1024×1024 image, six stories from the 25 November 2026 news cycle,
drawn as a newspaper front page: masthead **THE WORLD TODAY**, six panels,
each with a hand-drawn pictogram and a short truthful headline.

## The six stories

1. **HOUTHIS HIT RIYADH TOWER AGAIN — SECOND STRIKE IN A WEEK, 11 HURT**
   Saudi Arabia's tallest skyscraper in Riyadh was hit again in what Saudi
   officials called a terrorist attack — the second Houthi hit on the
   financial-district landmark in less than a week; 11 people were injured.
   Source: https://www.scmp.com/news/world

2. **CHINA PROBES TOP OFFICIAL — SHANGHAI CHIEF CHEN JINING HELD**
   China placed Politburo member Chen Jining, the Communist Party chief of
   Shanghai since 2022, under investigation for suspected severe violations
   of discipline.
   Source: https://beta.manilatimes.net/news/world/page/1814

3. **HUANG IN THERAPY — NVIDIA COMEBACK RALLY FADES**
   Nvidia's Jensen Huang said he is in therapy and made "some terrible
   mistakes" at an all-hands meeting a day after being reinstated as CEO;
   the comeback rally faded as Congress targeted Nvidia over China chips.
   Source: https://www.scmp.com/news/world

4. **RUSSIAN JETS IN KOREA ZONE — SEOUL: PLANES IN DEFENCE ZONE**
   Seoul said Russian military aircraft flew into South Korea's air defence
   identification zone, turning away just before violating airspace.
   Source: https://beta.manilatimes.net/news/world/page/1814

5. **HOSTAGE STANDOFF IN CARTAGENA — 25 HELD AFTER FAILED RAID**
   Gunmen held 25 people hostage in a supermarket in Cartagena, Colombia,
   after a failed armoured-car raid; police and troops surrounded the store.
   Source: https://www.scmp.com/news/world

6. **TRUMP HONOURS GIULIANI — MEDAL OF FREEDOM AWARDED**
   Donald Trump awarded Rudy Giuliani the Medal of Freedom, the highest US
   civilian honour, at the White House.
   Source: https://saachibaat.com/international-news/international-world-news-25-november-2026/

## How the image is made

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) draws the front page with its
own PNG encoder — no image library, no network, no randomness. Two runs
produce byte-identical output:

```
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py   # writes out/entry.png (1024x1024)
```
